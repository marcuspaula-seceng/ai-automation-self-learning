Conditional sums in a spreadsheet

SUMIF checks a criteria range and adds values from a corresponding sum range
when the criterion matches. Keep both ranges the same size. If there are no
matches, the result is zero.

In a fictional supply list, cells A2 through A4 contain Pens, Clips, and Pens.
Cells B2 through B4 contain 4, 7, and 9. The formula
=SUMIF(A2:A4,"Pens",B2:B4) returns 13 because it adds 4 and 9.
