# Weekly recall review

Use this worksheet at the end of the [one-week pilot](../pilot/one-week.md) or after any week of study. Keep your completed review with your study notes.

## Recall before checking

Choose three questions from an approved study note. Hide the answer key and write your answers from memory. Then check each answer against the note and its source. Give yourself one point for each correct answer and zero for each partial, incorrect, or blank answer. Record what was missing.

## Decide what to practice next

- **Misconception or gap:** What did I miss, and which source or example corrected it?
- **Next practice task:** What can I do independently to check that I understand it?
- **Next review date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **One workflow change:** What will I adjust about how I capture, practice, or review?

## Fictional example

- **Weekly recall:** 2 / 3. **Pilot baseline:** 1 / 3. **Pilot final:** 2 / 3.
- **Gap:** I tested the amount column when the condition belonged in the category column.
- **Next practice:** Write a conditional sum for another category and verify the total by hand.
- **Next review:** Two days after this review.
- **Workflow change:** Label the role of each range in every worked example.
